About

Counsel built on rigour, discretion & results

Ziad Roukoz is a Lebanese attorney and Managing Partner of A&A Associate Law Firm & Legal Advisors in the United Arab Emirates, and the principal of Roukoz Law Firm in Lebanon. His practice bridges civil-law tradition and the fast-moving commercial reality of the Gulf.

He brings extensive experience in construction law — with a specialty in FIDIC contracts — alongside general commercial law, banking, project finance and private equity. He specialises in international and domestic arbitration proceedings, with a particular focus on high-value construction and commercial disputes.

Before leading A&A Associate, he served as Counsel at AL-HARBI Law Firm and held senior associate positions at established Lebanese firms, advising businesses large and small as well as private individuals — always with the same standard: flexible, creative problem-solving delivered with professionalism.
